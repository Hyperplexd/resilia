# RESILIA: TEDx-style pitch (about 6 min)

**Title:** *"Burn It Down Before It Burns: Rehearsing Disasters Before They Happen"*

---

## Act 1: The hook (45 sec, no slides, black screen)

> "In July 2018, a fire reached the seaside town of Mati in Greece. Within about an hour, 104 people were dead. The fire wasn't the only problem. Nobody knew where it would go, and nobody had rehearsed what to do.
>
> *Hope is not a plan.*
>
> We rehearse fire drills for buildings. We rehearse flights in simulators. But for the whole city, we rehearse nothing. We just *hope*."

## Act 2: The insight (45 sec)

> "Pilots have a saying: *train hard, fly easy.* Cities have the opposite: they train zero and fly blind.
>
> It's not a question of *if*, but *when*. So I asked a different question: **what if we could burn the city a thousand times, safely, before it burns once?**
>
> Simulate twice, evacuate once. This is **RESILIA**."

## Act 3: Live demo (3 min, the core of the talk)

| Cue | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Landing / role pick** | "Two people live in every disaster: the one who decides, and the one who runs. RESILIA serves both." |
| 2 | **Resident: enter "12 Pine Street"**, map flies in and drops a marker | "A resident doesn't need a dashboard. They need one answer: *is my street safe?*" |
| 3 | **Digital twin: 3D buildings, vegetation, infrastructure** | "This is Cedar Ridge, a town sitting where homes meet wildland. Every building and every road is here. This is the *digital twin*." |
| 4 | **Hit Play on the fire sim; show the wind and dryness controls** | "Now the question: *what happens if a fire starts here?* Watch the front move. Now watch the smoke. It always travels farther than the flames, and that's what actually kills people." |
| 5 | **Scrub the timeline T+00 → T+30, 10x speed** | "Thirty minutes. Look at what's cut off by minute 15. Cities usually discover this by *living* it." |
| 6 | **Switch to Authority view** (only if Phases 5-8 are done: impact analytics, traffic lines turning red, evacuation) | "For the commander it's not a map, it's a decision engine. The road that turns red is the one you close *before* the fire gets there." |

**Stage tip:** Record the full demo as a backup video. Do the live demo only if the Wi-Fi is solid. OpenFreeMap tiles need the internet, so cache the map or pre-load the area.

## Act 4: Why now / why us (45 sec)

> "Wildfires are getting bigger, hotter, and closer to where we live. The tools to fight them are still *reactive*. Digital twins changed manufacturing and aviation. Cities are next.
>
> *The best way to predict the future is to build a place where you can practice it.*"

## Act 5: The ask and close (30 sec)

> "Today RESILIA is a what-if engine. Next, we plug in real wind, real terrain, and real fire-spread models. The goal: every municipality gets a flight simulator for its worst day.
>
> We can't stop every fire. But we can stop being surprised by them.
>
> *Don't wait for the spark.* Thank you."
